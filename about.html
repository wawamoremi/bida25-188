Our Story

Warm Corner of the city.

Wawa's Corner began as a simple idea, a small unhurried place where good coffee and quiet conversations could meet.It started as a corner shop and has since grown into a neibhourhood favourite but the heart of it has stayed exactly the same.

We source our beans from carefully chosen roasters, bake our pastries fresh each morning and prepare every drink as though it is being made for someone we care about.Whether you're stopping by for a quick expresso, spending and afternoon reading in peace or catching up with an old friend, there is always a corner for you to sit.

Come as you are and stay as long as you like.
